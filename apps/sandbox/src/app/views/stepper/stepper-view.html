Stepper

A map of a multi-step journey — "step 2 of 4" above a checkout. The application owns the journey (validation, routing, what "done" means) and hands the map one number; the component draws everything else: a list a reader counts, a marker per step, a check that is also a word, and aria-current="step" on the step that number names.
