Progress

A bar that reports how far along a task is. It is a <progress>: the role, the bounds and the value come from the element, and this library writes no aria-valuenow of its own. What it draws over it is a groove and a fill, because painting the element's own parts needs appearance: none — and that takes the engine's indeterminate animation away.
